The DevRel Foundation is in a governance transition. As we [shared in July](https://github.com/DevRel-Foundation/governance/discussions/165), the previous Steering Committee has completed its service, and the Linux Foundation is facilitating an open community process to shape the Foundation's next priorities, contribution paths, and leadership.

The center of that process is the **DRF Community Touchpoint**: an open call every other Wednesday where the community meets to reconnect, share challenges, and decide together where the Foundation can be most useful.

## What happens at a Community Touchpoint

Since September, Touchpoints have brought practitioners together to plan the 2027 DRF Unconference, discuss a framework for project donations, and explore how DevRel work changes when developers work through agents.

- **When:** every other Wednesday at 13:00 UTC, for 60 minutes. The next sessions are on November 11 and November 25.
- **Who:** everyone. You don't need a membership or a company affiliation.
- **Agenda:** each agenda is posted in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/DevRel-Foundation/governance/discussions/categories/announcements) before the call, and anyone can add topics.
- **Facilitation:** community volunteers take turns facilitating, with support from Linux Foundation staff.

Find every session, in your local time zone, in the redesigned [community calendar](/calendar). To join, register for the meeting with a free LFX account.

## Call for sessions: agent-assisted workflows for DevRel

At the October 14 Touchpoint, the community agreed to host short show-and-tell sessions about automated and agent-assisted workflows for DevRel work. Have you built or tried a workflow that:

- Updates a demo when a new product version ships and checks that it still works?
- Turns a talk recording into drafts for a tutorial, blog post, or social content?
- Prepares workshop exercises, tests the code, and puts the materials together?
- Collects content and event metrics for a recurring report?
- Helps answer developer questions, or turns recurring questions into documentation?

We want to hear how the steps connect, which tools and reusable skills are involved, what runs automatically, and where a person needs to review the work. What didn't work is just as useful. Any platform is welcome, including experiments and work in progress. You can also present a talk you gave somewhere else.

Sessions can be a 5 to 10 minute demo, a lightning talk, or a short discussion starter.

## How to submit

1. Go to the [session submission form](/community/touchpoints/submit).
2. Tell us what you want to share, the tools involved, and when you would like to present.
3. Community organizers will contact you by email to find a date.

Prefer to discuss your idea in public first? Comment on the [show-and-tell discussion on GitHub](https://github.com/DevRel-Foundation/governance/discussions/178).

## Help shape what comes next

The Foundation's next phase depends on community members who step forward to shape priorities, organize the work, and help deliver it. Joining a Touchpoint, presenting a session, or facilitating a call are all good ways to start.

Learn more on the [Community Touchpoints page](/community/touchpoints), and join the conversation on [Discord](https://discord.gg/G7CSTKZcuT).
